# Research and live SERP
Collect seed topics, product taxonomy, existing landing pages, target locale and conversion goals. Prioritize first-party Search Console queries, landing-page performance and existing cannibalization; then Ahrefs keyword/competitor data and DataForSEO current Google SERP snapshots. Use Keyword Planner when relevant for supplementary demand estimates. Record provider, market, language, retrieval date, metrics, URL and limitations for every dataset.

For each target query: document intent, dominant page types, organic result URLs, SERP features, content gaps, page overlap, difficulty estimates and business relevance. Search volume and difficulty are provider estimates, not absolute facts. Live SERP retrieval is preferred; if unavailable or stale, mark it explicitly.

Never infer a keyword's ranking potential from volume alone. Avoid presenting fabricated exact numbers or treating third-party AI detectors as Google's ranking signals. Prefer original insight, factual accuracy, helpfulness and user satisfaction.
