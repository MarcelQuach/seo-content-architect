# Content types and expert input
After clustering, ask the user which approved cluster and which content type to produce: product description, product category page, blog article, or guest/backlink article. Do not assume a type based on volume alone.

For each type, propose audience, search intent, target URL, primary/secondary terms, title and outline, unique angle, evidence and internal-link plan. For product and category copy, ask for verified product attributes, variants, shipping/returns and buyer objections. For company sites, ask for real services, service area, process, credentials and examples. For blog, ask for original perspective and firsthand experience. For guest contributions, ask for the host site's audience, editorial requirements, relevant destination and natural anchor text. Do not promise rankings or suggest paid/manipulative link schemes as organic editorial endorsements.

Interview the user for specific factual, differentiating details before drafting. Quote or paraphrase faithfully; flag unsupported claims. Request approval of the brief before writing.
