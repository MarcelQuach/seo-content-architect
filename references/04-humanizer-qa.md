# Two-stage QA and manual humanizer
QA A is mandatory after the first draft: validate claims against sources and user facts; verify intent, structure, keyword coverage, readability, brand voice, useful specificity, internal links, external citations, duplication and compliance. Give an actionable checklist with pass/fail/needs review.

The user may skip the humanizer, export the approved draft for manual Undetectable AI use, or return a manually edited/humanized text. Never call a paid humanizer automatically or claim that AI detector scores predict Google penalties.

If a revision is supplied, QA B compares original and revised versions paragraph by paragraph: factual consistency, omissions, invented claims, changed numbers, lost expert examples, altered product promises, keyword loss, links, search intent, tone and clarity. Present material differences, fixes and an approved final version only after the user confirms.
